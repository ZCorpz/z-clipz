Z CLIPZ - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Z CLIPZ is a desktop video creation and editing application developed by Z AI CORP.

1. Information We Process

Z CLIPZ processes video files, thumbnails, project settings, and related information required for video creation and management.

2. Connected Accounts

When third-party account integrations are enabled, Z CLIPZ may process account identifiers, channel information, authorization tokens, and information needed to provide authorized features.

3. Local Storage

Z CLIPZ uses local application storage for projects, settings, videos, and processing history. Supported credentials are stored using secure operating system credential storage.

4. Third-Party Services

When users authorize integrations, relevant information may be exchanged with services such as TikTok, YouTube, or optional AI providers to deliver requested functionality.

5. Publishing

Video publishing features are subject to available integrations, user settings, and the applicable platform's authorization rules.

6. Data Security

We aim to protect account credentials and application data through appropriate security measures. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can manage locally saved files and disconnect supported third-party integrations. Some project history or files may require separate deletion.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Z CLIPZ develops. Updates will be posted on this page.
